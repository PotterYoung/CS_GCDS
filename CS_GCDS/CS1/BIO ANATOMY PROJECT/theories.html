Scientific Theories:

ㅤ

Global Workspace Theory:

This theory suggests your brain stores all the necessary information and makes it available verbally when it is necessary.

It defines two main terms: "ignition” is the process where sensory information is broadcasted to the higher level areas of the brain, and “spiking” is a large amount of jolts of activity from neurons is necessary for ignition.

Ignition leads to consciousness, specifically a balance is necessary (having enough to perform ignition, but not too much otherwise the brain loses its ability to respond to inputs and produce reliable computations to reach consciousness).

ㅤ

Higher Orders Theory:

This theory suggests that a conscious mental state involves having higher-order mental representations of stimuli, usually in the higher levels of the brain responsible for cognition, to experience the world.

ㅤ

Integrated Information Theory:

This theory suggests phenomenal consciousness requires a full experience, and that nothing can be changed or separated or it would be a different experience.

ㅤ

Attention Schema Theory:

This theory suggests that there is a seperation between attention and awareness, and that they work seperately. Specifically that attention is that we are focused on in the world, while awareness is the model we have of our attention.

ㅤ

What does this prove?

On its own these theories just propose possibilities for how consciousness could work, and it is only through combining our understanding of these possibilties while researching further that we may find out how conciousness truly works.
